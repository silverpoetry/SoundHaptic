# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and constructive.
- Focus technical disagreement on evidence and reproducible behavior.
- Respect privacy when sharing logs, screenshots and device information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about safety, scope and compatibility claims.

Harassment, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information and unsafe instructions
are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the security contact described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens the project or its community.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
